# OpenParcelBox Brand Usage

This document gives practical examples for using the OpenParcelBox name, logo and related marks.

For the formal policy, see `TRADEMARK.md`.

## Allowed descriptive use

You may use the OpenParcelBox name descriptively and accurately.

Examples:

- “This project integrates with OpenParcelBox.”
- “Compatible with the OpenParcelBox API.”
- “A fork of OpenParcelBox named ExampleBox.”
- “Uses the OpenParcelBox MQTT specification.”
- “Works with self-hosted OpenParcelBox installations.”

## Not allowed without written permission

You may not use OpenParcelBox marks in a way that suggests official status, certification or endorsement.

Examples of use that require permission:

- “Official OpenParcelBox Lock”
- “OpenParcelBox Certified”
- “Powered by OpenParcelBox”
- using the OpenParcelBox logo on commercial hardware as a product brand
- running a hosted service under the OpenParcelBox name
- presenting a fork as the official project
- using the logo as your own app icon or product logo
- using confusing domains or service names that imply official status

## Fork naming

Forks must use clearly distinguishable names.

Allowed:

> “ExampleBox, a fork of OpenParcelBox.”

Not allowed:

> “OpenParcelBox Pro Cloud” for an unofficial hosted service.

## Compatibility claims

You may say “Compatible with OpenParcelBox API” if the statement is accurate and not misleading.

You may not say “OpenParcelBox Certified” unless certification has been granted.

## Domain names

Do not register or use confusing domain names that imply official OpenParcelBox status, such as names that look like official cloud, support, certification or product sites.

## Commercial use

Commercial use of OpenParcelBox code under open source licenses is allowed according to those licenses.

Commercial use of OpenParcelBox names, logos, certification marks or official compatibility claims is separate and requires permission where stated in the trademark policy.

Commercial vendors are welcome to apply for an official OpenParcelBox brand, compatibility or product license. Such a license may allow a product to be sold as an official OpenParcelBox product, official variant, OpenParcelBox Compatible product or similar approved offering.

No product may be marketed as official, certified, endorsed or licensed by OpenParcelBox until the applicable written permission has been granted and the required conditions have been met.

The exact access requirements still need to be defined, but they are expected to cover documentation, repairability, spare parts, local operation, interface transparency, security review and correct use of the OpenParcelBox marks.
