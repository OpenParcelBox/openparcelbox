# OpenParcelBox Trademark Policy

This policy explains how the OpenParcelBox name, logo and related project identifiers may be used.

OpenParcelBox, the OpenParcelBox logo, OpenParcelBox Cloud, OpenParcelBox Certified, OpenParcelBox Compatible, Powered by OpenParcelBox, openparcelbox.org and related marks are reserved project identifiers and trademarks of the OpenParcelBox project.

This policy does not claim that every mark is registered in every jurisdiction. It defines how the project permits use of its names, marks and identity.

## Why this policy exists

OpenParcelBox is open at its core, but the project identity must remain trustworthy.

The name and logo should help users identify official OpenParcelBox software, hardware, documentation, services, certification and partner integrations. They must not be used in a way that creates confusion, suggests false endorsement or makes unofficial products look official.

## What is allowed

You may use the OpenParcelBox name in a descriptive and non-misleading way, for example:

- to refer to the OpenParcelBox project
- to link to https://openparcelbox.org
- to describe that your project integrates with OpenParcelBox
- to state that your software uses the OpenParcelBox API
- to describe a fork, provided the fork uses a clearly distinguishable name
- to discuss compatibility, provided you do not imply official certification or endorsement

Examples:

- “This project integrates with OpenParcelBox.”
- “Compatible with the OpenParcelBox API.”
- “A fork of OpenParcelBox named ExampleBox.”

## What is not allowed without written permission

You may not use the OpenParcelBox name, logo or related marks in a way that implies official status, certification, partnership or endorsement without written permission.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- selling commercial hardware under the OpenParcelBox name
- operating a hosted cloud service under the OpenParcelBox name
- calling a product “Official OpenParcelBox Lock” without approval
- using “OpenParcelBox Certified” without certification
- using the OpenParcelBox logo as your own product logo
- using OpenParcelBox marks in a way that suggests partnership or endorsement
- registering confusing domain names, product names or service names
- presenting a fork as the official OpenParcelBox project

## Forks and modified versions

Forks and modified versions must use clearly distinguishable names. They may describe their relationship to OpenParcelBox, but they must not present themselves as official OpenParcelBox products or services.

A fork may say:

> “ExampleBox is a fork of OpenParcelBox.”

A fork may not say:

> “ExampleBox is the official OpenParcelBox cloud.”

## Compatibility statements

You may use “Compatible with OpenParcelBox” only as a descriptive statement and only if it is accurate and not misleading.

You may not use “OpenParcelBox Certified”, “Official OpenParcelBox”, “Powered by OpenParcelBox” or similar claims unless you have written permission or have completed the applicable certification process.

## Certification marks

OpenParcelBox Certified, OpenParcelBox Compatible and similar certification or compatibility marks may be subject to additional requirements, testing, approval and written permission.

## No trademark license in open source licenses

Open source licenses for code, documentation or hardware designs do not grant permission to use OpenParcelBox names, logos, certification marks or domain identity.
